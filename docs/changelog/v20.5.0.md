# CryptoEdge v20.5.0 — execution realism and research controls

## P0 Safety

- Candle closure is validated against the end of the timeframe, not candle-open timestamp.
- Portfolio and correlation-cluster risk are measured as loss at active stops.
- Traditional, equity, index, commodity, metal and forex instruments are excluded centrally.
- Orders persist actual maker/taker role, partial fills, VWAP and decision-to-fill latency.
- Restart regression covers an exchange position, orphan order and missing protective SL.

## P1 Replay

- Added deterministic decision → submit → accept → partial/full fill state flow.
- Limit touch can be pessimistic or seeded probabilistic; pessimistic is the default.
- Cancel requests have latency and may race with fills.
- Genuine 1m datasets can be downloaded on demand; missing 1m/L2 is never synthesized.
- Spread varies by major/alt profile and stress regime.
- Trade and L2 events can cap fills by recorded quantity.

## P2–P3 Research

- Added counterfactual filter attribution and monotonic OOS score calibration.
- Signals expose distance from invalidation, fill probability and adverse-selection score.
- Candidate quality combines expected net R, fill probability, adverse selection,
  invalidation quality and portfolio-cluster independence.
- No entry threshold was loosened.
